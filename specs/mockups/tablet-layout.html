The tablet band: 768 to 1023 pixels

Everything in the app shell — the hamburger, the drawer, the sidebar, the content padding and the frosted header — switches at one breakpoint, Tailwind's md: (768px). An iPad in portrait is 768–834px wide, so it gets the full desktop treatment on a screen that cannot carry it.

What I would do

Option A. Move the shell's breakpoints from md: to lg: — one consistent change across the hamburger, the drawer, the sidebar and the content padding — and make the header solid at every width. A tablet in portrait then behaves exactly like your phone, which is the layout you already use every day; a tablet in landscape is 1024px or wider, so it still gets the full sidebar. Nothing at 390px or 1180px moves.

Option B keeps navigation visible, but it buys that with unlabelled icons, and the whole reason this is worth fixing is that the tablet is short of room, not short of taps.
