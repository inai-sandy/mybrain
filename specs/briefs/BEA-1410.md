# BEA-1410 — Brief First 6/6: rebuild the nightly email agent the new way (the proof)

Part six of **Brief First**. Plan: <https://claude.ai/code/artifact/5c17fc65-a717-43b3-9bc6-4221968b0d94>
Linear: <https://linear.app/beakn/issue/BEA-1410>
Needs 1–5 (BEA-1405→1409). **Nothing else gets rebuilt until this one passes.**

## Why

"Nightly Important Email Summary" read **one** email a night for weeks and WhatsApped the owner
`"finished · 5 rows saved to Documents"` — a receipt, not the summary he asked for. He approved a
description in a chat; the form silently dropped the parts it had no box for. This piece proves the
new road on that exact agent, with him judging the real output.

## Build

Walk the whole new path, for real, once:

1. A fresh interview that **looks at his inbox before planning** and says what it actually found.
2. A brief he reads: his own words, what the tools really returned, and the AI's guesses in a
   different colour.
3. The exact WhatsApp message written out in the brief — grouped the way he asked, not a receipt.
4. His success sentence recorded as a hard check.
5. Codex builds the program from the brief plus the full transcript.
6. A **trial run** on his real inbox — real rows, the real message on screen, the real cost.
7. He judges. Create only if it is what he asked for.

## Acceptance (WHEN / MUST)

- WHEN the trial runs, it MUST read **all** the emails the brief asks for — not one, not the first
  page — and the run MUST show the true count.
- WHEN the trial produces its message, it MUST be the grouped summary described in the brief, NOT a
  "finished · N rows" receipt.
- WHEN the trial runs, nothing MUST be written to Documents or a sheet, and no message MUST be
  delivered unless the owner taps send.
- WHEN the success check is not met, Create MUST be refused and the reason shown plainly.
- WHEN the agent is created, it MUST run on the worker road (`useWorker: true`) and its first history
  entry MUST be the trial.
- The whole path MUST be done **without a single hand-fix from Claude Code**. If it needs one, this
  issue fails and the reason is a bug in 1–5, to be fixed there.

## Notes

- The old agent stays untouched until the new one passes; then the owner decides what happens to it.
- Never blind-delete anything: only items created by this run, by id.
- One real WhatsApp at most, to the owner's number, and only if he taps send.
- Report to him in plain English: what it read, what it sent, what it cost.
