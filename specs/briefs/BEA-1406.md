# BEA-1406 — Brief First 2/6: the brief screen (read it, fix it, approve it)

Part two of **Brief First**. Plan: <https://claude.ai/code/artifact/5c17fc65-a717-43b3-9bc6-4221968b0d94>
Linear: <https://linear.app/beakn/issue/BEA-1406>
Needs 1/6 (BEA-1405). Web only.

## Why

The owner has never been able to see what the AI actually filled in — only what it typed in the chat.
The two were printed in the same colour, in the same paragraph, and one of them was invented.
**This screen is the first of the two gates.**

## Build

**One screen, one column, readable on a phone.** Seven sections in order — What I want · Where it
comes from · What counts, what to ignore · What to do with it · When it runs · What "it worked" means
· If something goes wrong — plus a **Killed** section at the bottom.

- **Every line carries its tag**, and the three tags are visually distinct: *your words* · *looked* ·
  **my guess**. The guess tag must be the one the eye lands on.
- A `looked` line shows what was actually seen, and tapping it opens the real sample that proves it.
- **Edit any line in place.** An edited line becomes `origin:'owner'`.
- **Strike any line.** It moves to Killed, struck through, still readable. Un-strike puts it back.
- **Add a line** to any section.
- **Approve** — refusal reasons from the server appear in plain English beside the section that is
  missing something, not as a toast that disappears.
- The exact outgoing message (WhatsApp/Telegram) is shown as it will look, in a message bubble, not
  as a field label.

Use the house design system and existing components (`specs/COMPONENTS.md`) — no new spacing or
colour values.

## Acceptance (WHEN / MUST)

- WHEN the brief has an `ai` line, it MUST be visually distinguishable from an `owner` line without
  reading the text.
- WHEN the owner edits a line, it MUST become `owner` and the change MUST persist across a reload.
- WHEN the owner strikes a line, it MUST appear in Killed, struck, and MUST NOT be deleted.
- WHEN approve is refused, the reason MUST appear beside the offending section in plain English.
- WHEN a `looked` line is tapped, the real sample behind it MUST open.
- The screen MUST read correctly at **1180 and 390**, light and dark.

## Notes

- `UI_ROUTES` must point at the real brief route, and the run MUST be verified on that route —

- Plain English everywhere.
