What it will never do

Four refusals, on purpose. They are what let you leave it running without watching it.

01

Never acts without asking, when it cannot be undone.

Deleting, sending, changing a permission. It stops, tells you exactly what it is about to do and with which account, and waits. Everything else runs freely, so you see a question about once a week, not once an hour.

02

Never invents an answer to look busy.

When a source goes down, it writes nothing and says why. An empty result and a failed attempt look the same from outside. It never lets them look the same to you.

03

Never quietly gives you something worse.

When a limit is reached it stops and tells you. It does not switch to a cheaper, weaker AI to stay inside a budget.

04

Never reads your secrets.

Your vault is locked inside your browser before anything leaves it. The server stores unreadable text. Not even the labels reach the AI.
